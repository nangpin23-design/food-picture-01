# food-picture-01 — The Best-Looking Food Picture

A gourmet cheeseburger, shot like a professional food photograph: a tall
stacked burger on a glossy sesame brioche bun, melted cheddar dripping over
a thick seared beef patty, with fresh lettuce, tomato, red onion and
pickles, on dark slate with fries and a small bowl of sauce blurred behind
it. Warm golden light, shallow depth of field.

The image was generated as a single square photograph (800x800 PNG) and is
shipped in this repository; `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png` and
verifies the copy (existence, byte size, and SHA-256 hash).
